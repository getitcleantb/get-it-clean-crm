Privacy Policy

Get It Clean Tampa Bay LLC · Last updated November 12, 2026

Get It Clean Tampa Bay LLC ("we", "us") provides power washing, soft washing and window cleaning in the Tampa Bay area. This policy explains what we do with the information you give us when you ask for a quote, including through our Facebook and Instagram ads.

What we collect

When you request a quote we collect your name, phone number, email address, property address, the services you are interested in, and when you would like the work done. If you contact us by text, phone or email we keep a record of those conversations.

How we use it

We use your information only to contact you about your quote, schedule an estimate or job, send appointment reminders, send invoices, and follow up about our services. We may text you at the number you gave us, including automated follow-up texts about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop texts, or tell us and we will stop.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run our business (for example our texting, email, scheduling and payment providers), and only so they can do that work for us, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep customer and quote records for as long as we need them for our business and legal records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, or to stop hearing from us, text or call (727) 270-8945.
